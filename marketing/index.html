The terminal

Dense enough for a desk, honest enough to show its work.

Every number on screen is server-authoritative. Every signal comes with the reasoning behind it. Every state — live, paper, halted, stale — is labeled, never just a color.

Server-timed countdown

Never the browser's clock. Entries restrict at 60s, disable at 20s, resting orders cancel at 5s — enforced where it counts.

Live order book

Best bid, best ask, spread, and depth, streamed from one backend feed — not a hundred browsers hammering the exchange.

Grok signal card

Fair probability, market probability, edge, and confidence — plus the reasoning, so it's never a black box.

19-check risk engine

Staleness, liquidity, slippage, daily loss, position limits, kill switch. One rejection, one clear reason — every time.

Paper before real

Realistic fills, slippage, and fees — every strategy proves itself on paper before it ever touches a wallet.

Trading prediction markets involves financial risk. AI-generated signals from Grok are estimates, not guarantees — GrokPulse's risk engine can reject any signal and defaults to PASS whenever data is stale, missing, or uncertain. Past performance does not guarantee future results. GrokPulse is non-custodial: it never holds your funds and cannot process a withdrawal, because your assets never leave your own wallet.
